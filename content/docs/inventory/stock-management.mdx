---
title: Stock management
description: Monitor on-hand stock and respond to low-stock risk.
---

Set low and critical stock thresholds in configuration, then use dashboard alerts and warehouse-manager notifications to act before stock reaches a critical level.

Before monitoring stock, create the [products](/docs/inventory/products) and [warehouses](/docs/inventory/warehouses), then record inventory against the appropriate product and location. An empty product catalogue or a warehouse record alone does not establish a stock balance. Review the location and quantity after a sale or transfer.
