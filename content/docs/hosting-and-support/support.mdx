# Training and support

The subscription includes standard onboarding assistance, end-user training, user manuals, remote support during official business hours, technical assistance, and standard software, security and feature updates during the active term.

Custom development, extensive data correction, on-site support, bespoke integrations and non-standard migration may be quoted separately.
