---
title: Onboarding flow
description: Follow the SORKOL CRM activation journey from account creation to live operations.
---

Use this checklist to track setup. The [Get started guide](/docs/get-started) contains the instructions for each step.

| Order | Action | Ready to continue when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Create an account](/docs/get-started/create-account) and verify the work email. | You can sign in through the organisation login page. |
| 2 | [Complete the company profile](/docs/get-started/verify-company) for review. | The organisation is ACTIVE. |
| 3 | [Choose a subscription](/docs/get-started/choose-subscription) and complete payment. | The subscription is active; a pending receipt is not yet confirmation. |
| 4 | [Configure the workspace](/docs/get-started/configure-workspace). | Roles, products, stock, prices and payment settings are ready. |
| 5 | Test the operational flow. | Sale, payment, receipt and stock records match the intended result. |

Account registration is self-service. Company review and manual-payment confirmation are separate approval steps; completing the registration form alone does not enable live operations.
