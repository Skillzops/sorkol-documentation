# Separation of duties

Assign access according to job responsibility rather than convenience. For example, sales users can create quotes and sales without payment-credential access; warehouse users can manage stock without sensitive financial reports; invoicing officers can manage invoices and receipts without changing access settings; and installers can view assigned tasks without unrelated portfolios.

Review role membership and high-risk permissions regularly.
