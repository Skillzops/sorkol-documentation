---
title: Get started
description: Activate your SORKOL CRM workspace and prepare it for operations.
---

Follow these steps in order. Email verification, organisation approval and subscription activation are separate steps.

1. [Create your organisation account](/docs/get-started/create-account) and verify your email.
2. [Complete your company profile](/docs/get-started/verify-company) for review. You can sign in while approval is pending, but operational modules remain locked.
3. [Choose a subscription](/docs/get-started/choose-subscription) and confirm that it is active.
4. [Configure your workspace](/docs/get-started/configure-workspace), then test your first sale and payment.

Use an account authorised to set up the organisation. Keep access to your work email and have your company phone number, CAC number and CAC document ready for company review.

Already using SORKOL? The [platform overview](/docs/get-started/platform-overview) explains where to find the operational guides.
