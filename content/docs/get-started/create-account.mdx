---
title: Create your organisation account
description: Create a SORKOL tenant workspace for your organisation.
---

1. Open [SORKOL sign-up](https://energy.sorkol.com/client/signup).
2. Enter your company name, work email, password and password confirmation.
3. Select **Create account**.

![Create a SORKOL organisation account](/images/docs/get-started/create-account.png)

Open the verification message sent to your work email and confirm your email address. Then use **Continue to login**, or the organisation-specific login URL shown after registration, to sign in.

Verifying your email does not approve your organisation or activate a subscription. You can complete your company profile while organisation approval is pending.

**Next:** [Verify your organisation](/docs/get-started/verify-company).
