---
title: Choose a subscription
description: Select a plan and complete payment to activate your workspace.
---

**Before you begin:** Complete [organisation verification](/docs/get-started/verify-company). Use an account with access to **Subscriptions & Billing**.

1. Open **Subscriptions & Billing**.
2. Review the current subscription first. If it is already active, continue to workspace setup rather than starting another payment.
3. Choose the plan and billing period, then review the checkout summary.
4. Complete the selected payment process.

![Subscription selection](/images/docs/get-started/choose-subscription.png)

For online checkout, wait for payment confirmation and check the subscription status in SORKOL. For a manual bank transfer, use the generated payment reference and upload the receipt as instructed. Uploading a receipt does not activate the plan immediately; it awaits administrator confirmation.

Continue when the subscription is active. If payment is pending, check the existing payment and subscription history before paying again.

**Next:** [Configure your workspace](/docs/get-started/configure-workspace).
