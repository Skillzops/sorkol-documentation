---
title: Configure your workspace
description: Prepare users, commercial rules and operational settings before go-live.
---

**Before you begin:** Your organisation and [subscription](/docs/get-started/choose-subscription) must be active. Use an administrator account for configuration.

Prepare the workspace in this order:

1. [Create roles](/docs/users-and-access/roles) and [assign users](/docs/users-and-access/users) to the work they will perform.
2. [Create products](/docs/inventory/products), [create warehouses](/docs/inventory/warehouses) and [record stock](/docs/inventory/stock-management) for items you will fulfil.
3. [Configure price books](/docs/configuration/price-books) for One-Time or PAYGO offers.
4. [Configure invoicing](/docs/configuration/invoicing) and [payment gateways](/docs/configuration/payment-gateways) for your collection process.
5. Configure [commissions](/docs/configuration/commissions) if used. For connected devices, configure the [OEM integration](/docs/configuration/oem-integrations) before vending.
6. Test a quote, sale, payment, receipt and stock movement using controlled test records. For PAYGO, also check the repayment and device flow.
7. Enable the [automation](/docs/configuration/automation) you need after checking its settings. Apply [branding](/docs/configuration/branding) when ready.

Check that each test has the intended customer, amount, stock location and access permissions before starting live operations.

**Next:** [Run sales](/docs/sales), or use the [platform overview](/docs/get-started/platform-overview) to find another workflow.
