After [workspace setup](/docs/get-started/configure-workspace), use these guides for daily work:

| Area | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| [Sales](/docs/sales) | Prepare quotes and create One-Time or PAYGO sales. |
| [Inventory](/docs/inventory) | Maintain products, stock locations and fulfilment. |
| [Finance & payments](/docs/finance) | Record payments, review invoices and receipts, and reconcile balances. |
| [PAYGO](/docs/paygo) | Follow instalments, devices, tokens and repayments after a PAYGO sale. |
| [Configuration](/docs/configuration) | Set pricing, invoicing, stock rules, commissions and automation. |
| [Users & access](/docs/users-and-access) | Assign roles and permissions. |
| [Integrations](/docs/integrations) | Connect payment, OEM and Odyssey services. |
| [Security](/docs/security) | Review access and audit activity. |

Tasks and reporting are also available in the application. Menu visibility depends on your role and enabled modules. If a required menu is missing, ask your workspace administrator to review your access.
