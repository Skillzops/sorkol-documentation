# Verify your organisation

After verifying your email, sign in and open **Company** settings. Enter the organisation profile, upload the company logo and provide the required registration or verification documents.

![Company profile settings](/images/docs/get-started/company-profile.png)

Submit the completed profile for SORKOL approval. Activation continues after the information is reviewed.
