---
title: Verify your organisation
description: Complete your company profile and submit it for approval.
---

**Before you begin:** [Create your account and verify your email](/docs/get-started/create-account), then sign in using your organisation's login page.

1. Open **Settings → Company** using **Open Company settings** in the pending-approval banner.
2. Complete your company profile, including the company phone number, CAC number and CAC document requested for review.
3. Save the required information and follow the review instructions shown in the app.

![Company profile settings](/images/docs/get-started/company-profile.png)

While your organisation is **PENDING**, operational modules remain locked. Email verification and company review are separate: a verified email alone does not unlock the workspace. Continue when the organisation status is **ACTIVE**.

**Next:** [Choose a subscription](/docs/get-started/choose-subscription).
