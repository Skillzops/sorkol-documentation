# SORKOL CRM Documentation

SORKOL CRM is a managed cloud platform for customer management, sales, Pay-As-You-Go operations, payments, invoicing, inventory, commissions, field operations and reporting.

![SORKOL CRM dashboard](/images/docs/live/dashboard.png)

## Get started

Set up your organisation, verify your account, choose a subscription and configure your workspace.

## Sales

Learn how to manage quotations, One-Time Sales and Pay-As-You-Go Sales.

## Configuration

Configure pricing, payments, invoicing, inventory, commissions and integrations.

## PAYGO

Manage financing, instalments, devices, vending, repayments and portfolio monitoring.

## Security and administration

Manage users, roles, permissions, audit records and organisation-wide configuration.
