---
title: Integrations
description: Connect approved payment, OEM and external services to SORKOL.
---

SORKOL supports approved payment gateways, dedicated virtual accounts, SMS and email services, OEM device and vending APIs, REST APIs and Odyssey. Availability can depend on subscription, provider approval and technical assessment.

![Integration setup](/images/docs/live/tenant-integrations.redacted.png)

Set up each service before relying on it in an operational workflow. See [payment gateway setup](/docs/configuration/payment-gateways), [OEM setup](/docs/configuration/oem-integrations) or [Connect Odyssey](/docs/integrations/odyssey).
