# Audit logs and traceability

Use supported audit records to review user and role assignments, permission changes, customer activity, quotes and sales, payments, invoices, receipts, product movements, price-book and commission versions, integration settings, automation history and failed jobs.

Transaction histories help identify what changed, who performed an action and when it occurred.
