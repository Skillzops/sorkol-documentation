# Service terms

SORKOL CRM is a standardised SaaS platform. Custom development, bespoke reports, workflows, integrations, extensive migration work and other services outside the selected plan may require a separate agreement, cost and timeline.

Third-party integrations depend on external provider availability, compatibility, approval and service stability. The client is responsible for the accuracy, legality and completeness of uploaded information. Client-specific security, hosting, residency, retention or compliance requirements must be agreed before becoming contractual obligations.
