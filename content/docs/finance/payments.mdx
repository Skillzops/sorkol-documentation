---
title: Record payments
description: Capture full payments, deposits and repayment activity.
---

Record direct-sale payments, PAYGO initial deposits and subsequent repayments. Where supported, payment gateway or approved dedicated virtual account integrations can assist with reconciliation. Maintain customer payment history and outstanding-balance visibility.


Select the correct customer and sale or invoice before recording a payment. Check its status and reference before entering another record, especially when a gateway notification may already have recorded it. Then [reconcile the balance](/docs/finance/reconciliation).

**Continue:** [Manage receipts](/docs/finance/receipts).
