# Plans & billing

SORKOL CRM is provided as Software-as-a-Service. Your organisation owns its business data; the application, source code, architecture and related intellectual property remain the property of Skillz Systems Ltd.

![Subscriptions and billing](/images/docs/live/subscriptions.redacted.png)

Your subscription becomes active after organisation approval, plan selection, completed payment and confirmation by SORKOL or the approved payment process. Renew before expiry to retain uninterrupted access, managed hosting, support, updates, maintenance and backup services. The checkout terms and any signed agreement govern the selected plan.
