# Client responsibilities

Complete the in-platform onboarding actions accurately: verify the organisation, provide valid documents, select and pay for the subscription, configure operating settings and protect credentials. Maintain accurate, lawful and authorised uploaded data; required third-party accounts and API credentials; compatible devices, browsers and reliable connectivity; and appropriate user access.

Review workflows and reports before operational use, and remove access promptly as staff responsibilities change.
