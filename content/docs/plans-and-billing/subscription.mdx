# Subscription and renewal

The subscription becomes active after organisation approval, plan selection, completed payment and confirmation by SORKOL or the approved payment process. Renewal is required for uninterrupted access, hosting, support, updates, maintenance and backup services.

The August 2026 service offering lists a standard reference annual fee of ₦3,500,000. The selected checkout terms and any signed agreement govern when they differ.

Standard subscription scope includes platform access, managed hosting and database infrastructure, subscribed-user licensing, tenant provisioning, One-Time and PAYGO features, configuration, roles and permissions, standard updates, maintenance, daily automated backups, business-hours support and onboarding/training.
