---
title: Quotes
description: Prepare and track customer quotations before finalising a sale.
---

Create quotations using the active product and price-book variants. A new price book affects future quotes and sales while preserving values recorded on historical transactions.

![New Quote modal](/images/docs/live/quotes-modal.redacted.png)


Before creating a quote, create the [product](/docs/inventory/products) and activate the [price book](/docs/configuration/price-books) you intend to use. Check the customer, offer and amount before finalising the quote.

**Continue:** [One-Time Sales](/docs/sales/one-time-sales).
