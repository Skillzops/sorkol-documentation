# PAYGO Sales

Use a PAYGO sale where a customer pays a deposit and completes the remaining balance through scheduled instalments. Configure the initial payment, recurring amount, duration and payment mode in the active price book.

The sale can produce a master invoice, repayment history, reminders, device registration, token generation and portfolio monitoring.
