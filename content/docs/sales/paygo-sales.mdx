---
title: PAYGO Sales
description: Create an instalment-based sale with device and repayment operations.
---

Use a PAYGO sale where a customer pays a deposit and completes the remaining balance through scheduled instalments. Configure the initial payment, recurring amount, duration and payment mode in the active price book.

The sale can produce a master invoice, repayment history, reminders, device registration, token generation and portfolio monitoring.

Before starting, prepare the customer, product and active PAYGO price-book variant. Configure an OEM connection only where the device needs it. After creating the sale, follow the [PAYGO operating flow](/docs/paygo); check existing invoices and schedules before creating additional records.
