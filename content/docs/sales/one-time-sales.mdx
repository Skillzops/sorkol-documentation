---
title: One-Time Sales
description: Record a direct sale without a PAYGO instalment schedule.
---

Use a One-Time Sale when a customer pays the full agreed amount, or when you record a direct sale without an instalment schedule. Select the customer, product and sales agent; create the quote or sale; record and reconcile payment; then fulfil stock and assign an installer where required.

SORKOL can generate receipts, deduct inventory and calculate configured flat or percentage commissions.


Before recording a sale, check the customer, active price-book offer and available stock. After recording payment, [review the payment and receipt](/docs/finance/payments) and confirm the stock movement. A sale record alone is not proof that payment has completed.

**Continue:** [PAYGO Sales](/docs/sales/paygo-sales).
