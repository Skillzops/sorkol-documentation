# One-Time Sales

Use a One-Time Sale when a customer pays the full agreed amount, or when you record a direct sale without an instalment schedule. Select the customer, product and sales agent; create the quote or sale; record and reconcile payment; then fulfil stock and assign an installer where required.

SORKOL can generate receipts, deduct inventory and calculate configured flat or percentage commissions.
