Use SORKOL to connect customer finance, repayments, device operations and portfolio monitoring in one workspace.

![PAYGO devices workspace](/images/docs/live/devices.redacted.png)

## Operating flow

Prepare the product and active PAYGO price-book offer, then create the sale. Review its initial payment, invoice and instalment schedule before adding records so the deposit or schedule is not duplicated. Register and activate a compatible device where needed, receive repayments, then monitor the portfolio.
