---
title: Tokens and vending
description: Activate devices and use approved OEM vending connections.
---

After registering the device, use the approved OEM integration to generate tokens, activate devices and review vending status. Test OEM credentials before operational use and restrict credential management to authorised users.


Before issuing a token, check the customer, device identifier, OEM connection and the payment or activation period being applied. Review the request result before retrying to avoid duplicate vending requests.

**Continue:** [Repayments](/docs/paygo/repayments).
