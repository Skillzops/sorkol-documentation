---
title: Instalments
description: Manage scheduled customer payments for PAYGO sales.
---

Use the configured duration and recurring amount to manage instalment schedules. SORKOL provides outstanding-balance and repayment-history visibility and can support automated unpaid-payment and renewal reminders.


Start from the [PAYGO sale](/docs/sales/paygo-sales) and review its existing schedule. Do not create a second schedule or record the initial deposit twice.

**Continue:** [Devices](/docs/paygo/devices).
