# Configure invoicing

Choose how invoices and receipts are numbered and delivered. Configure PAYGO master invoices, direct-sale master and sub-invoices, receipt generation and receipt email delivery. Set prefixes, tax details, currency, bank details and payment terms.

Sequence tracking is read-only, helping preserve traceable document numbering.
